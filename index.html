About the Founder

Built from scratch.
Taught from experience.

I came from nothing and figured it out the hard way. No roadmap. No financial education. I made the mistakes so my clients don't have to. Everything I teach is built from what actually works — not theory.

"Most people drift through their 20s reacting to whatever life throws at them. The ones who build lives they're proud of aren't smarter or luckier. They just decided to aim."

I work with young adults in their 20s — college or not — who feel lost, stuck, or unsure of their next move. I give them the direction, financial foundation, and personal roadmap to build a life they're genuinely proud of.

Who This Is For

Sound familiar?

Society gives young adults two options — go to college or figure it out yourself. Either way, nobody teaches you how to actually build a life.

Profile A

The Crossroads Kid

You're in college, questioning everything. No clear direction. You're not even sure the path you're on is the right one — and nobody around you has real answers.

Profile B

The Stuck Graduate

Degree in hand, wrong job or no job. You feel like everyone else has it figured out except you. You're out of patience with the guesswork and ready for a real plan.

Profile C

The Independent Starter

You skipped college, you're working, you're hustling — but you have no idea if you're doing this right. Nobody is showing you how to build a real financial and career foundation.

"The most important decade of your life shouldn't be left to pressure and guesswork."
